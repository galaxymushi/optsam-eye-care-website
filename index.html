Optsam · Seeing is believing

Seeing is believing.

Optsam began in 2017 as an idea: a platform for eye-health outreach, awareness and community service. In 2019 it became the business identity through which J.M. Samwel practised optometry.

That practice joins two things that are often separated: the clinical care of an eye examination and the craft of an optical workshop. The aim is simple — understand how you see, explain what can be done, and make the spectacles that follow.

01 What we do

Primary eye care,
end to end.

From the first vision test to the finished pair of spectacles — and a referral when specialist care is the right answer.

  1. 01

    Eye examinations

    Vision and eyesight assessment with an optometrist, with a clear explanation of what the results mean and what to do next.
  2. 02

    Prescription spectacles

    Corrective lenses prescribed and dispensed for your eyes, with frames selected and measured for comfort.
  3. 03

    Optical workshop

    A workshop for making and fitting spectacles — the practical side of turning a prescription into a pair you can wear.
  4. 04

    Contact lenses

    Contact-lens fitting and supply, including the lens types Optsam lists as available.
  5. 05

    Referral & eye health

    When a condition needs specialist care, Optsam refers patients to an ophthalmologist rather than stretching the limits of primary eye care.

Services listed here are those Optsam describes in its own materials. Availability can change — please confirm when you enquire.

02 · The workshop

Where a prescription becomes something you wear.

Optsam’s optical workshop is where spectacles are made and lenses fitted to frames. It is the practical half of the practice — and the reason a prescription does not stop at a piece of paper.

04 Why Optsam

A practice, not
just a counter.

  1. 01

    Optometrist-led care

    Optsam was founded by J.M. Samwel, an optometrist. The practice examines eyesight, prescribes corrective lenses and refers patients to an ophthalmologist when specialist care is needed.

  2. 02

    Care and craft in one place

    The optical workshop makes and fits spectacles. That means the prescription, the lenses and the frame are considered together.

  3. 03

    A documented community record

    From Ukonga and Chang’ombe to Migombani Primary School, Optsam’s community eye-care work is documented in its own photographs and posts. See the record.
