06 / 06

Facts, sources and photo credits.

A website should be judged by what it can support. This page records where the facts came from, what could not be verified, and which permissions are still outstanding.

Research standard

Optsam’s own published material is the primary source for this site. Where a claim could not be checked independently, it is described that way.

Sources checked on 17 October 2026: the Optsam blog and its post feed, the official Blogger profile, Optsam’s printed service flyers, and the Instagram and YouTube accounts linked from Optsam’s profile.

No fact was taken from an unrelated directory, review site or stock image caption. No independent check of professional registration, qualifications, opening hours or current service availability was possible.

01 · Claims

What the site says,
and why.

Each entry records the claim, the exact source, and the limits of verification. A “self-described” status means the source is Optsam itself, not an independent register.

  1. 01

    The business identity is “Optsam Eye Care and Optical Workshop Store”.

    The name is used across Optsam’s blog, its 2021 service flyers, and its 2026 official statement.

    StatusVerified from Optsam’s own publications

    Source Optsam official statement, 6 September 2026

  2. 02

    Optsam began as an idea in 2017; the business identity was established in 2019.

    Optsam’s professional biography says the name was formed from “Opt” (Optometrist) and “Sam” (Samwel), and that the business identity was established in 2019. The blog profile describes the business as “licensed and certified … since November 2019”. The licence itself was not independently verified.

    StatusSelf-described; licence not independently verified

    Source Optsam professional biography, 11 September 2026

  3. 03

    Optsam was founded by Samwel John Mushi, professionally known as J.M. Samwel, a Tanzanian optometrist.

    The biography gives the full name Samwel John Mushi. The profile and 2024 documentary script use J.M. Samwel / Samwel John. The profile says he studied at the School of Optometry at KCMC, Moshi, after field work at CCBRT in 2017. These qualifications were not independently verified with KCMC or CCBRT.

    StatusSelf-described; credentials not independently verified

    Source Optsam Blogger profile

  4. 04

    Optsam provides eye examinations, prescribes and dispenses corrective lenses, assesses eye conditions, refers to specialists, and runs an optical workshop.

    The profile describes “examining eyesight, dispensing and prescribing corrective lenses, manage and diagnose eyediseases or refering to specialists.” The 2021 flyer lists eye testing, appointment scheduling, a workshop for making spectacles, referral to an ophthalmologist, and treatment with medicine and spectacles. The 2024 documentary script describes vision examination, therapeutic spectacle dispensing, identifying and managing eye conditions, and referral.

    StatusVerified as Optsam’s stated scope

    Source Optsam documentary script, 15 April 2024

  5. 05

    The optical workshop makes and fits spectacles, and Optsam lists therapeutic lenses, cosmetic lenses, branded frames, contact-lens fitting and optical accessories.

    The 2024 documentary script’s store section describes those product categories. The 2021 workshop post is titled “Karakana ya kutengenezea miwani aina zote” (a workshop for making all kinds of spectacles). Instagram captions also refer to lens fitting and the workshop.

    StatusVerified as Optsam’s stated offering; current stock not verified

    Source Optsam optical workshop post, 7 April 2021

  6. 06

    Optsam is located on Mianzini Street, Mbagala, Dar es Salaam.

    The address appears on Optsam’s printed service flyer. The Blogger profile lists “Mbagala, Dar es salaam, Tanzania”, and a 2020 post is titled “Optsam office at Mbagala, Dar es salaam”. No exact map pin or building number was published.

    StatusStreet and area verified from Optsam’s flyer; map pin unconfirmed

    Source Optsam service flyer post, 19 May 2021

  7. 07

    The published telephone number is +255 755 477 225.

    The number appears on Optsam’s Blogger profile, both 2021 service flyers, and Instagram posts from 2020 and 2021. We could not place a call to confirm that it is currently active, or confirm that it is enabled for WhatsApp.

    StatusPublished by Optsam; current activity not verified

    Source Optsam Blogger profile

  8. 08

    The published email address is optsamtz@gmail.com.

    The address is printed on Optsam’s “Offering eye care and optical services” flyer. It is not listed on the Blogger profile. Deliverability and monitoring were not verified.

    StatusPublished by Optsam; deliverability not verified

    Source Optsam service flyer post, 19 May 2021

  9. 09

    Optsam’s community work includes Ukonga, Magereza Hospital, Chang’ombe, Migombani Primary School and Kilamba Primary School.

    These activities are documented in Optsam’s own posts and in the 2026 professional biography. The 403-person figure from the Chang’ombe camp is reported by the Tanzanites Lions Club in a post republished by Optsam. The May 2024 mobile-clinic post describes a project and does not confirm that a service was delivered.

    StatusDocumented by Optsam; third-party figures attributed

    Source Optsam professional biography, 11 September 2026

  10. 10

    In 2026, J.M. Samwel transitioned from private practice into public service; Ask Optsam continues as a personal, non-commercial eye-health education initiative.

    The official statement says the business identity represented the commercial side of his practice and that he has now officially transitioned into public service. It describes Ask Optsam as separate from his government role and not a substitute for clinical care.

    StatusVerified from Optsam’s own statement

    Source Optsam official statement, 6 September 2026

  11. 11

    Opening hours, prices, stock and staff are not published.

    No opening hours, price list, frame inventory or staff list was found on the blog, profile, flyers or linked social channels checked. The site therefore omits them and asks visitors to confirm by phone or email. This is an absence of information, not a statement that the business is closed.

    StatusNot found in official sources — owner confirmation required

    Source Optsam blog and linked official channels

What was left out

No invented
reassurance.

This website deliberately does not include:

  • testimonials, reviews or star ratings;
  • patient numbers, success rates or “years of experience” totals;
  • awards, certifications or professional registrations that were not published;
  • opening hours, prices or a frame inventory;
  • staff names or photographs beyond those published by Optsam;
  • a founder portrait or any image of a person Optsam did not identify;
  • Facebook or TikTok links, because no official account could be verified;
  • claims that Optsam provides emergency or specialist surgical care.

If you are the owner and can confirm any of these, they can be added accurately — with the supporting detail on this page.
