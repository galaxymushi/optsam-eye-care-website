Frames, lenses and workshop care.

Optsam’s optical workshop is where a prescription becomes a pair of spectacles: lenses fitted to frames, and frames chosen for the person wearing them.

Inside the workshop

Made to be
worn.

A good pair of spectacles is not just a prescription in a frame. It is a set of measurements, a choice of lens, a frame that sits properly — and the workshop work that brings them together.

Optsam describes an optical workshop for making spectacles and fitting lenses. That workshop sits alongside the eye examination, so the clinical decision and the practical result are part of the same practice.

The website does not publish a frame catalogue, stock list or prices. Those details change, and guessing would not help you. Ask when you enquire and Optsam can tell you what is currently available.

From prescription to fitting

Three practical
stages.

The exact process depends on your prescription and the options available. Confirm the details when you enquire.

  1. 01

    The prescription

    Your eye examination establishes what correction you need. Bring any current spectacles or previous prescription with you.

  2. 02

    The frame and lens

    Discuss the frame and lens options currently available. The choice is about fit, comfort and how you use your eyes — not just appearance.

  3. 03

    The fitting

    The workshop makes and fits the spectacles. Ask about turnaround and what to do if the fit needs attention afterwards.
