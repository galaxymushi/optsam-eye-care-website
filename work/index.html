A dated record

Where the work
has taken us.

This is a selection from Optsam’s published record, not a complete list of every activity. Dates are the dates Optsam documented.

  1. 2020

    Ukonga, community settings

    Optsam documents an Integrated People-centred Eye Care screening at Ukonga, and eye-health activity with TAG Ukonga Church, retired prison officers in Ukonga and Rotary events.

  2. Feb 2021

    Rotary optometry at Magereza Hospital

    A visit by Rotarians who are optometrists at Magereza Hospital, Ukonga. Photographs from the visit were published by Optsam in April 2021.

  3. Jun 2021

    Sokeny Polyclinic, Mabibo

    Optsam publishes a post titled “9th June SOKENY POLYCLINIC Mabibo, Dar es salaam”, with images from the clinic.

  4. Sep 2021

    TMHS Polyclinic, Mikocheni

    A program-based consultancy with TMHS Polyclinic, Mikocheni, is recorded in a September 2021 post.

  5. Oct 2021

    Chang’ombe, Temeke

    Optsam republishes the Tanzanites Lions Club account of a free vision, diabetes and hearing camp at Gwambina Lounge. The club reports that 403 people were screened. Optsam was there; the figure belongs to the club’s account.

  6. Oct 2022

    World Sight Day, Migombani Primary School

    Free comprehensive eye screening for teachers and students at a government school in Tabata Segerea, Dar es Salaam.

  7. May 2024

    Kilamba Primary School, Mbagala Charambe

    Optsam publishes a post about the Swift Charitable Mobile Eye Clinic project, with photographs of the mobile clinic and pupils at the school.

Reading this record carefully

What this page is — and isn’t.

It is a selection of community activity published by Optsam between 2020 and 2024. It is not a client list, a claim of endorsement by any named organisation, or a promise that the same programmes are running today.

Some photographs show patients, pupils and staff. They are reproduced from Optsam’s public posts at the owner’s request for this website. Written consent and formal permission should be confirmed before a public launch; the sources and photo credits page records the outstanding checks.

A note on the Chang’ombe figure

The figure of 403 people screened is reported by the Tanzanites Lions Club in an account republished by Optsam. It is not presented as an independently audited Optsam statistic.
