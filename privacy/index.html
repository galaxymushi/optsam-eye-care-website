What this website does not do

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It does not use third-party tracking scripts, social-media embeds, embedded maps or embedded videos. It does not create an account for you or store your enquiry on a server controlled by this website.

The enquiry form

The enquiry form runs in your browser. When you choose “Continue in email” or “Continue in WhatsApp”, it prepares a message using the details you entered and opens the relevant app on your device. Nothing is transmitted by the website itself. You can review the message and decide whether to send it.

If you send the message, it is delivered through your email provider or WhatsApp and received by Optsam at the contact details published on this site. Those services handle the message under their own privacy terms.

Please do not send sensitive medical details

Use the form to ask about an appointment, services or eyewear — not to send medical records, test results or detailed health information. If you have an urgent eye problem, go to the nearest hospital or emergency service rather than using this website.

Hosting and technical data

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process standard technical request information (such as an IP address, browser type and the page requested) in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. This website does not add analytics and does not use that information to profile visitors. The final hosting provider’s own privacy notice should be checked before launch.

External links

Links to Google Maps, Instagram, YouTube, the Optsam blog and WhatsApp take you to services with their own privacy policies. This website does not control those services and does not receive information from them unless you choose to interact.

Photographs of people

Some photographs on this site show patients, pupils and staff, and were published by Optsam on its own blog. The owner should confirm written permission and consent before public launch. If you appear in a photograph and want it removed, contact Optsam using the details below.

Children

This website is general information, not a service directed at children. Please do not submit a child’s personal details through the enquiry form without the consent of a parent or guardian.

Your choices

Because this website does not store enquiries, there is no website record to access, correct or delete. If you have sent Optsam an email or WhatsApp message and want it corrected or deleted, contact Optsam directly and ask.
