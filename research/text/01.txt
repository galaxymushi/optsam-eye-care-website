 About Ask Optsam ni jukwaa lisilo la kibiashara (Non-profit platform) nchini Tanzania linaloongozwa na mtaalamu wa macho, J.M. Samwel born 15/4/1997 (Optometrist). Lengo letu kuu ni kutokomeza upofu unaozuilika na kusaidia jamii inayokabiliwa na uono hafifu kupitia utoaji wa elimu sahihi, awareness, na taarifa za afya ya macho zilizothibitishwa kitaalamu. Hatufanyi biashara ya miwani; tunajikita 100% katika kuisadia jamii kupata ushauri salama wa kitabibu. •Ask Optsam is a non-profit public health education platform founded by J.M. Samwel born 15th April 1997 (Registered Optometrist) in Tanzania. We provide expert-verified eye care awareness, blindness prevention guidelines, and low vision information to eliminate preventable vision loss in Tanzania.
